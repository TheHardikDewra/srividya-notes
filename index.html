In December 2010, before the sadhana that began that January, Om Swami wrote out the whole of his Sri Vidya practice by hand — sixteen sheets covering the krama from first purification to final visarjana. He later published the scans and a typed transcript openly on os.me, for readers of Thirteen Months in the Himalayas who came looking for them. This is that material, made navigable.

The nine āvaraṇas are laid on a Śrī Chakra you can actually touch; the forty-one steps are a sequence you can follow; every line carries its IAST; and his own handwriting sits one tap away from the typed text.

    On the mantra

    These notes carry the three kūṭas of the Pañcadaśī inside the nyāsa lines. They are held back here by default. Om Swami published them himself, so nothing is hidden that he has not already opened — but a mantra of this order is received by dīkṣā from a living Guru, not picked up from a page. Reveal them only if that is already your practice.
